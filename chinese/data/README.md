# Chinese word lists

`hsk1.tsv` to `hsk6.tsv` hold the words of HSK levels 1-6 in the 2025 syllabus
(cumulative targets about 300 / 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,600 / 5,400 words), most frequent first.
Each word appears once, at the lowest level that lists it.

Columns: simplified, traditional, pinyin, part of speech, other readings.

- Word lists, levels, readings, parts of speech and frequency order come from
  [Complete HSK Vocabulary](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary) (MIT License,
  notice below). Dictionary meanings from that project (CC-CEDICT, CC BY-SA) are not copied here;
  each app writes its own short meanings.
- The reading was picked automatically when a word has several. Words with other readings list
  them in the last column; check them against the official syllabus when building a part.
